## Why
Provide interactive, on-demand historical Dia de Sorte analysis with visible progress and retained results.

## What Changes
Use the local official workbook, implement separately selectable contest-analysis strategies, display graphical or tabular results, and preserve responsiveness. Correct and document legacy defects. This is a draft pending full specification and readiness; candidate scoring, betting and simulations are deferred.

## Capabilities
### New Capabilities
- `interactive-strategy-analysis`: local data, strategy menu, progress and results.

### Modified Capabilities
None.

## Impact
Rust CLI, spreadsheet data adapter, terminal interface, strategy modules and tests; PentaPRO five-pillar contracts. Windows, Linux and macOS are intended targets. Source freshness and strategy-specific details remain pending.
