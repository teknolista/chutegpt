# Domain

Dia de Sorte uses seven distinct drawn numbers from 1–31 and a separately drawn month. See [research](../../docs/dia-de-sorte-referencia.md) for citations, rules, mathematical assumptions and open questions. Official rules, prices and prize schedules must be associated with their applicable dates. The current historical strategy catalog and data semantics are in [interactive analysis](interactive-strategies.md); other strategies remain future scope.

Legacy research: [Portuguese inventory](../../docs/analise-estrategias-legado.md) describes implemented historical metrics, scoring, generation, and known uncertainties. It is research, not an accepted feature contract; suitability and predictive value remain unverified.
